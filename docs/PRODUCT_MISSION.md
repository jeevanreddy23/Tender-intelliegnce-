# Product Mission

Build and continuously improve an NSW geotechnical opportunity intelligence
system that helps STS Geotechnics identify work early and act with evidence.

The product must present three distinct views:

1. **Active Geotechnical Tenders** — verified procurement opportunities that
   can be acted on now.
2. **Early Geotechnical Leads** — current projects likely to need future ground
   engineering, but without a verified active geotechnical tender.
3. **Historical and Awards Intelligence** — expired, closed, awarded, and
   completed records used for buyer, supplier, competitor, and scope analysis.

The default dashboard is Active Geotechnical Tenders. It must not show expired
or closed tenders, irrelevant procurement, unverifiable records, duplicate
cards, interstate or New Zealand records, or historical awards.

The system optimizes for high recall during ingestion and high precision in the
user-facing active feed. Source records remain attributable and auditable even
when excluded from the default dashboard.
